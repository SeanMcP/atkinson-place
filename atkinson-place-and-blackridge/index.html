Atkinson Place and Blackridge

Blackridge is a housing development that straddles the boroughs of Wilkinsburg, Churchill, and Penn Hills. According to the Blackridge Civic Association, only five houses on Atkinson Place are considered part of Blackridge.

Every house in the 1800 block is excluded.
