title: "Atkinson Place and Blackridge"
---

Blackridge is a housing development that straddles the boroughs of Wilkinsburg, Churchill, and Penn Hills. According to the Blackridge Civic Association, only the five houses in the 1700 block of Atkinson Place are considered part of Blackridge and eligible for membership.

Residents of Atkinson Place have participated in Blackridge-wide events such as the annual yard sale and neighborhood block party.
