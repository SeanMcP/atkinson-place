title: Eastridge Library Reopens after 2024 renovations
---

Eastridge Library reopened on Wednesday May 22nd after two months of renovations. Raised areas in the former Biography, Teen, and DVD sections were removed, the carpet was replaced, and the walls were repainted.

The Wilkinsburg Public Library is hosting a reopening celebration on Saturday June 1st from 11:00am to 4:00pm. All are welcome.
