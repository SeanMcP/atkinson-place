The annual Blackridge Block Party will be held on Saturday September 14 from 5:00pm to 10:00pm on Orlando Place between Yorktown and Georgetown.

Come for food, libations, fun, music, and dancing in the street!

Please bring a chair, covered dish, beverage, and/or your favorite snacks to share with your neighbors.

If you can help by lending a tend table or chairs, setting up, or cleaning up call or text Anne (412) 862-1492 or Kelly (412) 610-2198.
