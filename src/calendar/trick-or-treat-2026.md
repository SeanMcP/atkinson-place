hours: 5:00pm - 8:00pm

The Borough of Wilkinsburg will celebrate Halloween on October 31st with Trick or Treating from 5:00pm to 8:00pm. Turn on your house or porch light to let families know that you are participating.

For trick or treaters: it's a good idea to wear some reflective clothing or walk with a lit flashlight.
